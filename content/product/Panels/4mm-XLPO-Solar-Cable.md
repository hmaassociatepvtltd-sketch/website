  # 4mm² XLPO Single Core Photovoltaic DC Cable

  The 4mm² XLPO Solar DC Cable is designed for string wiring between solar modules and inverters in residential and commercial solar installations, providing optimal conductivity, low resistance, and 25-year environmental durability.

  ## Technical Specifications

  * **Conductor Material**: Flexible tinned annealed copper wire (Class 5 stranding)
  * **Insulation & Sheath**: Electron-beam cross-linked XLPO (Halogen-Free Flame Retardant)
  * **Rated Voltage**: 1.5kV DC (1500V DC rated)
  * **Continuous Current Capacity**: Up to 55A current rating
  * **Thermal Performance**: Ambient temperature range -40°C to +90°C (Conductor temp up to +120°C)
  * **Environmental Resistance**: UV, ozone, moisture, hydrolysis, acid, and alkali resistant
  * **Standards Compliance**: TUV EN 50618 / IEC 62930 approved for 25-year service life
