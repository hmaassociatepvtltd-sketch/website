  # Pylontech US3000C 3.55kWh LiFePO4 Lithium Battery Pack

  The Pylontech US3000C is one of the world's most widely deployed 48V Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) battery modules for home energy storage and commercial UPS backup.

  ## Key Performance Specifications

  * **Nominal Capacity**: 3.55 kWh (48V / 74Ah).
  * **Usable Capacity (95% DOD)**: 3.37 kWh usable capacity per module.
  * **Long Cycle Life**: ≥ 6,000 cycles at 95% Depth of Discharge (25°C).
  * **Modular Rack-Mount Design**: Easily stack up to 16 modules in parallel (56.8 kWh total capacity).
  * **Smart Internal BMS**: Monitors voltage, current, temperature, and cell balance automatically.
  * **Inverter Protocols**: Pre-configured CAN/RS485 communication with Solis, GoodWe, Nitrox, Victron, and Deye inverters.
