  ## Key Technical Features & Specifications


  * **Flexible Modular Capacity**: 6.14 kWh nominal capacity per pack (51.2V /
  120Ah). \* **Parallel Modularity**: Connect up to 12 battery packs in parallel
  for up to 240 kWh total storage. \* **Compact Outdoor Installation**:
  IP65-rated weatherproof wall-mount or floor-standing installation. \* **High
  Energy Density & Efficiency**: >96% round-trip efficiency with zero
  maintenance. \* **LiFePO4 Thermal Safety**: Advanced prismatic cells with
  thermal runaway protection and internal BMS monitoring. \* **Remote Firmware
  Upgrades**: Over-the-air (OTA) BMS firmware updates via cloud telemetry.
